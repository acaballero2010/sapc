## 2. Key Psychometric & Predictor Recommendations

1. **Inclusion of Protective Factors**:
   - Extracurricular involvement (clubs, sports, leadership) must offset raw academic deficits as a buffer against dropout.
2. **Dual-Layer Financial Assessment**:
   - Differentiate between **objective income** and **subjective financial stress** (e.g. debt, emergency medical expenses).
3. **Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) Compliance**:
   - Ensure PHQ-9, GAD-7, and qualitative counselor notes remain strictly confidential and accessible only to registered guidance personnel.
